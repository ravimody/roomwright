# Review personas

People who might use roomwright, directly or through a coding agent. Read any change to the docs (COOKBOOK, the design
guide, QUICKSTART, MODELING, the README, the household template) as each of them, and as an agent helping them. Nothing
should assume one country, culture, taste, budget, device, skill level or amount of time; where a fact differs by
region, say so; where something is taste, say whose.

These are composites built to test assumptions, not descriptions of how people in these places live: most real
households mix habits from many places, and plenty of them break the pattern their city suggests.

Questions to ask of a change:
- Would it mislead or exclude any of them (a word only one country uses, a habit stated as a rule, a device or skill
  they don't have, more time than they have)?
- Does it leave a path for them (no scan, chat only, a quick pass, a local maker, a rented home, a power cut)?
- Is evidence (safety, accessibility, sleep-light research) kept apart from taste, and taste left to the person?
