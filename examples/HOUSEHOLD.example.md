# Household profiles (examples)

Three made-up households, to show how different the answers can be. Write your own from the template that
`roomwright init` puts in `HOUSEHOLD.md`; agents read it before proposing anything, and it overrides the engine's
design guide, which is only guidance.

## A. A bright flat in a hot city

- **Where and who:** a large city in a hot, humid climate. Conversation in English, reports as PDFs in English. Metric. Two adults, two school-age children and a grandparent; the grandparent has the final say in the shared rooms.
- **The home:** owned apartment; free to paint, drill and change fixtures. Power cuts most days: an inverter runs the ceiling lights and fans in the living room and bedrooms. Ceiling fans in every room, split air conditioners in the bedrooms. Furniture placement follows the family's traditional rules (recorded against true north). Gatherings of 20–30 people a few times a year.
- **Buying:** tight budget, local currency. A local carpenter builds most storage and beds from drawings; upholstery from a neighbourhood shop; small items from online marketplaces.
- **Light and style:** bright, even, cool white (about 6000 K) from ceiling lights and tube lights is what feels right; one warm lamp by the grandparent's chair. Polished stone floors. Colourful and full: family photos and festival decorations stay.
- **Working together:** images in chat, a PDF for the family to discuss; quick passes, a full check only before the carpenter cuts anything.

## B. A small rented flat with floor seating

- **Where and who:** a dense city in a mild climate with cold winters. Conversation and reports in the person's first language; data keys stay in English. Metric. One adult who works from home, a cat.
- **The home:** rented: no drilling, no painting, the landlord's ceiling light stays. Earthquake-prone area: no tall furniture over the bed or the door. Shoes off at the door; floor seating and a low table in the main room.
- **Buying:** small budget; secondhand first, a little new from a chain store.
- **Light and style:** one cool-white ceiling light that dims by remote, plus a paper floor lamp for the evening. Minimal and calm; the person wants as few things on the floor as possible.
- **Working together:** a phone-friendly page; one weekend, quick depth.

## C. A family house with an older relative

- **Where and who:** a suburb in a cold climate. English; feet and inches for rooms, centimetres are fine for furniture. Two adults, a teenager, and a parent in their eighties who uses a walker and has low vision.
- **The home:** owned house; changes allowed, including new switches and grab rails. Reliable power.
- **Buying:** mid-range budget; new for anything the parent leans on, antiques welcome elsewhere. Delivery up one flight of stairs.
- **Light and style:** warm white (2700–3000 K) in the living room in the evening, but well-lit routes, steps and the parent's reading chair (bright and even matters more than mood). Traditional style with a few modern pieces.
- **Working together:** a local HTML report and a printed PDF in large type for the parent; safety items first in every report. Fully verified depth for the parent's rooms.
